## Plan v1.2: staff portal, applications with tracking IDs, jobs from the web (2026-11-14)

Plan: `docs/PLAN_v1.2_PORTAL.md`. Migration `0003_portal.sql` (78 base tables).

- **Staff portal** at `/admin` with its own sign-in (`/admin/login`), second factor, navigation, dashboard (queue, counts, recent applications), Applications (filters, search, detail, status change with public or private note, internal note, timeline), Opportunities (now with "where can people apply"), Web listings (sources, last run, refresh, hide), Organisations, Users, Audit log. Every staff write is audited.
- **Apply on the site**: per opportunity `apply_mode` = `external` / `site` / `both`. A signed-in person applies with a consent tick; the profile and eligibility result at that moment are stored for the reviewers. Each application gets a tracking ID `C2A-XXXX-XXXX`; one application per person per opportunity (applying again returns the same ID). My applications page with timeline and withdraw; public Track page (ID only, no personal data, rate limited).
- **Jobs from the web**: connectors for Arbeitnow and Remotive (no key needed) and Google Programmable Search (off until `GOOGLE_CSE_API_KEY` and `GOOGLE_CSE_CX` are set). Listings are cached (title, company, place, 500-character snippet, original link), refreshed lazily every 6 hours when the public feed is read or from the portal, and always shown as "From the web, not verified". Staff can hide any listing.
- Verification: 9 + 6 new unit tests, a 6-test TiDB suite (apply once, duplicate apply, public tracking without personal data, permissions, transitions, private notes, audit rows, withdraw, apply_mode, feed upsert/hide/failure), and a walk through a real browser: register, apply, tracking ID, public track by a lower-case ID with spaces, staff sign-in with MFA, status change shown on the public tracking page, web listings (316 real jobs fetched).

## Plan v1.3: suggestions, interest alerts, richer home (2026-11-14)

Plan: addendum in `docs/PLAN_v1.2_PORTAL.md`. Migration `0004_interests.sql` (80 base tables).

- **Suggestions ("For you")**: a transparent weighted score, not a black box. Signals: interests the person sets (kinds, keywords, places), activity (view 1, search 1, web job click 1, save 3, track 4, apply 5, half-life 21 days, 90-day window), eligibility against the saved profile, freshness and urgency. Items already saved, tracked or applied are left out. Every suggestion shows why (reason chips). Web jobs are matched on the same keywords and learned words. Pure scoring in `modules/recommend/score.ts`; database side in `service.ts`.
- **Alerts in the app**: job match (score at or above 4.5, once per opportunity), deadline reminder for saved or tracked items (72 h and 24 h slots), application update when staff change a site application (only steps marked visible). Created when the person opens the site (throttled to 10 minutes), when staff publish (fan-out to people with alerts on) and on status changes. A bell with the unread count, a Notifications page and an Interests page. Email waits for a provider; a scheduler (S-6) will later make reminders independent of visits.
- **Privacy controls**: "Personalise with my activity" switch (off stops recording and ignores history), "Clear my activity", both tables deleted with the account (registry), activity never shown to staff.
- **Home page** redesigned: hero with live preview cards and counters, search, calls to action, stat band, categories, for-you (or a sign-up teaser), closing soon, latest, web jobs, six feature cards, steps, FAQ, call to action. Mobile header fixed to one line.
- Bugs found by the tests and the browser walk-through and fixed: Bangla words were split at vowel signs in keyword matching (marks are not letters), the interests form sent a field the API refused, web job snippets from double-encoded HTML showed raw tags.
- Verification: 14 scoring unit tests, 9 TiDB tests (preferences, activity de-duplication, personalise off, clear, automatic save/track recording, suggestions differ by person, explicit interests, cold start, alert dedupe and read state, reminders per slot, publish fan-out to the right person only, application update notification, deletion with the account); real browser: set interests, saw reasons, bell "1 unread", notification in Bangla.

## Plan v1.4: employers (recruiters) with their own portal (2026-11-14)

- **Role name: Employer.** Sign-up asks "Find opportunities" or "Hire (employer)"; employers give a company name (website and phone optional). Employers are not staff (no MFA, normal sessions) and hold only `employer.manage`.
- **Employer portal** at `/employer` (own sign-in `/employer/login`, English only): dashboard, My posts (jobs, internships, circulars), create/edit, send for review, withdraw, close; applications to their own posts with the same status workflow, notes and tracking IDs; company profile. An employer sees and changes only their own posts and the applications to them.
- **Review rule**: employer posts are never published directly; staff (verifier or above) publish them. The official link is not required for employer posts. 15 posts per day per employer.
- Migration `0005_employers` (employer profile table, `opportunities.owner_user_id`); privacy registry covers both.
- Tests: 7 TiDB tests (sign-up types, profile, isolation, staff approve/close, applications and notifications, no access to staff routes, rate limit); 88 TiDB tests in total. Browser: employer sign-up, dashboard, create post, send for review, phone width without overflow.
- Bug found in the browser: `/employer` was redirected to a locale prefix; the middleware now leaves it alone like `/admin`.
- Owner admin: `admin123` is below the app's own rule (12 characters, plus MFA for staff), so no such account is created. Use `create-admin` with a strong password from `.env.local`.
- Not done: staff UI to mark a company verified; employer email notifications (no provider).

## Plan v1.5: more job sources, sponsored posts, push, notification board, security (2026-11-19)

- **Web jobs from many companies**: besides Arbeitnow and Remotive, the feed now reads Jobicy, Remote OK, Himalayas and the public career pages of 14 companies (Greenhouse and Lever job boards). The list of hosts is fixed in code (no user-supplied URLs). The list is ordered by how well each job fits the person, then spread out so one company or one source cannot fill a screen.
- **Not done on purpose: training models on third-party datasets.** Ranking stays a transparent weighted score (ADR 2026-11-14); copying other sites' data to train a model would raise licensing and privacy problems and gives worse, unexplainable results at this size. Revisit with real usage data.
- **Sponsored posts (demo plan)**: an employer asks to promote a live post (3, 7 or 14 days), staff approve or decline, an active one is shown, always labelled "Sponsored", on the home page and Explore to the people it fits best (rotating among the best few). Views and clicks are counted. **No payment is taken**: a real payment provider needs the owner's approval first.
- **Push notifications**: a person or employer turns push on per device (`/en/app/settings`, the notifications page, employer notifications) and chooses which kinds they want. Unread notifications are sent as web push (VAPID) within about 20 seconds, once each, in the person's language. Subscriptions are only accepted from real push services (SSRF guard), removed when a device is gone, and deleted with the account. Works on Android and desktop browsers; iPhone needs the site added to the Home Screen (Safari 16.4+).
- **Notification board** (`/admin/announcements`, now "Notifications"): staff send a message to job seekers, employers, everyone, people interested in one kind, or one person by email; history with audience and reach. Employers have their own notifications page with an unread badge.
- **Staff sign-in link removed** from the public footer (staff use `/admin` directly).
- **Security**: Content-Security-Policy, Permissions-Policy, COOP and (when hosted) HSTS on the web app; the service worker does no caching. Known: `pnpm audit` reports a high advisory in zod < 4.6.6 (memory use on oversized constrained collections). We use zod 3, request bodies are capped at 1 MB and every array has a maximum, so exposure is limited; the move to zod 4 is a separate, larger change.
- **Found while testing**: `/sw.js` was being redirected to a language prefix by the middleware (push would never have registered); sign-in and register crashed for signed-in people; checkboxes were too small for fingers.
- Tests: new `diversify` and source-parser unit tests, 5 TiDB tests for sponsored posts, push and the board. A layout audit script (`scripts/layout-audit.js`) checked overflow and tap targets on 360, 768 and 1440 px wide screens.
- Not done: email provider, real payments, scheduler for background alerts while the free instance sleeps (alerts and push go out while it is awake), Google search connector (needs keys).

Totals at last run: unit tests api 194, eligibility 73, web 10, others 47; TiDB suite 93 tests; `tsc` clean; ESLint clean.
